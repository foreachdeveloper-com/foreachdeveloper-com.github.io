02 Why forEach

Because nobody starts out knowing how. You start by poking at something you love until it does what you want, and it goes faster when someone has left the source open for you, and somebody else is happy to explain it.

  1. [0]

    The first loop

    I learned to code because I wanted to change the rules of a game. No classes, just forums, late nights, and a lot of broken builds. Still my favourite way to learn.

  2. [1]

    2009 · Venezuela

    I started EscuelaWeb and spent years teaching web development. The best moment was always the same one: somebody's first loop running and the look on their face.

  3. [2]

    Community, out loud

    In RubyVE, the Ruby community back home, I organised our podcasts and got people on the mic to share what they'd learned. That's when I understood: a community is a loop where everyone gets a turn.

  4. [3]

    A new home in Chile

    New country, same habit. Nearly two decades now of distributed systems, edge devices and cloud, carried from one side of the continent to the other.

  5. [4]

    Now, after bedtime

    There's a tiny human at home, so most commits land once the house goes quiet. Everything I build here stays open, for each developer who wants to read it, fork it, or learn from it.
